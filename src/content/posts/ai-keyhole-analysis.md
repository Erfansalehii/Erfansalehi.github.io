Finding and analyzing keyholes in noisy, low-contrast X-ray images is challenging and time-consuming. My master’s research at Brandenburg University of Technology Cottbus-Senftenberg explored how an AI-based pipeline could automate this analysis for laser beam welding.

## The problem

Low contrast and imaging noise make it difficult to isolate the keyhole consistently. Manual analysis also limits the amount of data that can be processed efficiently.

## The pipeline

I worked across the complete workflow: preparing the data, developing and training the model, generating predictions, segmenting keyhole regions, and extracting geometric information from the results.

During ongoing evaluation, the algorithm reported more than 95% accuracy. Beyond that headline metric, the project demonstrated how computer vision can uncover meaningful patterns across large imaging datasets much faster than manual inspection.

## Continuing the work

Reliable validation remains essential, particularly for scientific images with challenging edge cases. Further work focuses on refining the system and evaluating its behavior across more varied welding conditions.
