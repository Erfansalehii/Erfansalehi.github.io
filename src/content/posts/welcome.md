## Writing a new post

Create a Markdown file in `src/content/posts/`. Add the title, description, publication date, and tags in the frontmatter at the top of the file. Everything below the frontmatter becomes the article.

## Keeping a draft private

Set `draft: true` while you are working. Drafts are excluded from the blog index, generated article routes, and sitemap.

## Publishing naturally

You can ask: “Publish a blog post about my latest project.” Your portfolio manager can draft the Markdown file, summarize the preview, and leave the changes ready for your approval before deployment.
