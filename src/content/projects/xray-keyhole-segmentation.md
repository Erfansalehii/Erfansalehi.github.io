This research project focused on the development and validation of an AI-based algorithm for segmentation and geometric analysis of the keyhole in low-contrast X-ray images of laser beam welding.

I handled the complete pipeline, including model development, training, prediction, segmentation, and analysis. The project reported more than 95% accuracy during its ongoing evaluation and demonstrated how AI can accelerate analysis that is challenging and time-consuming to perform manually.
