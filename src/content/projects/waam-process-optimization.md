This project explores prediction and control of beam geometry in Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing. The software combines two connected capabilities:

- Automated detection of sudden errors and outliers in weld depth, width, and area against a rolling baseline, with an interpretable report for flagged regions.
- AI-driven optimization of wire-feed and travel speeds across a ten-layer build to target a stable five-millimeter wall width.

A live dashboard visualizes the learning curve and parameter adjustments, making the optimization process easier to inspect.
